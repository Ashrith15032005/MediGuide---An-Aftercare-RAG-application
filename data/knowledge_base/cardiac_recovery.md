## Diet

- Do: Follow a heart-healthy diet — low saturated fat, low sodium, high fiber.
- Do: Include omega-3 rich foods (fish, flaxseed) unless otherwise advised.
- Don't: Avoid trans fats, fried foods, and excess red meat.
- Don't: Limit sodium to under 1500-2000 mg/day to reduce fluid retention.

## Activity Restrictions

- Follow your cardiac rehab program's graded activity plan — do not exceed
  prescribed exertion levels.
- Avoid heavy lifting (>5 kg) and strenuous activity for at least 6 weeks
  post-surgery, or as advised by your cardiologist.
- No driving for 4-6 weeks after cardiac surgery, or until cleared by your doctor.
- Gradual walking programs are typically encouraged starting soon after discharge.

## Medication Timing

- Take blood thinners, beta-blockers, and other cardiac medications exactly as
  scheduled — do not skip doses.
- Report any unusual bruising or bleeding if on blood thinners.
- Do not stop cardiac medications abruptly without consulting your doctor.

## Wound / General Care

- Keep sternal/chest incision (if applicable) clean and dry.
- Watch for clicking or shifting sensations in the breastbone (post cardiac surgery).
- Monitor daily weight — sudden weight gain can indicate fluid retention.
- Attend all scheduled cardiac rehab and follow-up appointments.

## Red-Flag Symptoms — Seek Immediate Medical Attention

- Chest pain, pressure, or tightness
- Shortness of breath, especially at rest or when lying down
- Rapid or irregular heartbeat
- Sudden weight gain (e.g., >1 kg in a day) or significant swelling in legs/ankles
- Fever, or redness/discharge from a surgical incision
- Dizziness, fainting, or confusion
